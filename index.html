Personal Knowledge Graphs

Dependency-graph-style sites people have built to map out what they know — ideas as nodes, connections as edges. Hosted wherever their makers chose to host them.

Cards with a public GitHub repo pull a live "updated" date from GitHub. Everything else shows the date it was added to this list by hand — there's no way to check "last updated" generically across Vercel, custom domains, and everything in between.
